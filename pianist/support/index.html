Connecting your piano

Over USB

Connect the piano's USB port to your iPhone or iPad with a cable, or through Apple's USB-C or Lightning to USB adapter. Pianist finds it on its own: the Piano button, a keyboard at the bottom of Home, turns from gray to the app's color.

Over Bluetooth

Turn on your piano's Bluetooth MIDI. In Pianist, tap the Piano button, then Connect Bluetooth Piano, and choose your piano. Pair it there rather than in the Settings app.

No piano?

Listen, reading and editing all work without one. Learn needs a piano to hear what you play.

Scores

Pianist opens MusicXML files (.musicxml and .mxl), which MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, Dorico and most notation apps export. Import them from Files, open them from another app, or drag them onto the library. Browse Songs offers public-domain songs to download.

Pianist Pro

  • What it adds: Learn and Edit on every piece, Keep Going, New Score, and as many pieces as you like. Without Pro, the library holds 2 pieces of your own besides the bundled samples, and Learn and Edit are free on the samples.
  • Plans: monthly, or yearly with a 2-week free trial, or once for life. All are shared with your family through Family Sharing.
  • Restore purchases: in Pianist, open Settings, then Pianist Pro, then Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel a subscription: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds: Apple handles them at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your library stays on your device, in its backups. See the privacy policy.
