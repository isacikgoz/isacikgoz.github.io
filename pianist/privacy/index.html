Pianist Privacy Support

Privacy Policy

Pianist collects no personal data. Your music and your practice stay on your device.

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Nothing you play, import or write is sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your library: the scores you import, download or write, your markings, your practice history and your settings. Pianist keeps them in its own storage on your iPhone or iPad. They're part of your device's backups, if you have backups turned on, and leave with the app if you delete it.

Your piano

Pianist reads the notes you play on a piano connected over USB or Bluetooth MIDI, to follow and mark your playing. It does this on your device, as you play. Bluetooth is used only to connect to your piano.

Browse Songs

When you download a song in Browse Songs, Pianist fetches the score from the OpenScore Lieder collection on GitHub. Like any web request, it reaches GitHub from your device's internet address. Pianist sends nothing else with it. GitHub's handling of web requests is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Purchases

Pianist Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Pianist never sees your payment details or who you are. The app only learns whether this Apple Account has Pro, from Apple, on your device.

Children

Pianist collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with its date.
