Pianist

A piano coach for iPhone and iPad. It reads your sheet music, listens to your piano, and helps you learn a piece bar by bar.

  • Learn one hand or both. Each step waits for the right keys, wrong notes are marked, and with Keep Going the music plays on with you after a mistake.
  • Play it through at tempo and see how the notes, rhythm and steadiness went, and which bars to work on.
  • Listen to any piece at any tempo, with a cursor that follows the music.
  • Bring your music: MusicXML from any notation app, public-domain songs to download, or a score you write yourself with your finger, Apple Pencil or your piano.

Pianist works with digital pianos connected over USB or Bluetooth MIDI. It's in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
