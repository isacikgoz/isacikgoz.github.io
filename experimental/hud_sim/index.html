    View

    The world: a cartoon Teldrassil at twilight turning and sliding round you, its trees, rocks, moonwells, lanterns and mushrooms solid; five creatures wander near your starting spot. Enter the timber lodge through its open doorway to see its floor plan on the minimap. Your pet follows you (a wolf for a beast, a voidwalker for a demon), and your selected target stands ahead.
